限界: 祝日にふだんの平日ダイヤで走る路線もあり、「ふだんどおり」は誤りとは限りません。標準のservice_id（「平日（月～金）」など）だけのデータは、仕様上、国内の経路検索事業者が祝日を考慮します（表の括弧内）。それ以外の利用者（海外の地図アプリなど）にはファイルの内容がそのまま届きます。比べる日は祝日・年末年始・お盆を避けた同じ曜日の最も近い日です。
Limits: some routes really do run their weekday timetable on holidays, so "ordinary" is not always a mistake. For files that use only GTFS-JP's standard service_ids (the count in brackets), the spec says domestic route-search providers apply the holidays themselves; every other reader (map apps abroad, for example) gets what the file says. The ordinary day compared is the nearest same weekday outside holidays, the New Year break and Obon. Place names are records of fact, not judgements.
